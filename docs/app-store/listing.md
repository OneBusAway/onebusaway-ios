## What's New

This is a big release. It adds an Apple Watch app, shows availability of bikes, scooters, and on-demand transit (in supporting regions), greatly improves accessibility support throughout the app, and much more.

- Apple Watch app: See bookmarks, nearby stops, and their arrivals on your wrist. Your bookmarks sync from your iPhone.
- iPhone Duo support: if you're one of the lucky few sporting an iPhone Duo on launch day, rest assured that OBA will work great on it from day 1.
- Micromobility support: In supporting regions, you'll be able to see nearby scooters and bikes from Lime, Bird, and other providers.
- Bike Mode: Arrival estimates can use your cycling speed. The stop header shows walking and biking times separately.
- On-demand transit: In regions that offer them, see dial-a-ride and flexible services, along with their booking deadlines.
- Trip bookmarks: Bookmark a trip together with the stop where you'll get off.
- Destination alerts: Get alerted as you approach your stop. You can set an alert from the Stop page.
- Report a ghost bus from the trip page, if a bus shown in the app never arrives.
- Directions to or from here on any stop page.
- Weather on the map now shows the conditions as well as the temperature.
- Multiple windows on iPad.
- Home Screen widget: The widget now supports stop bookmarks.

## Description

When's my bus? OneBusAway tells you, in real time, at every stop in your region: on your iPhone, iPad, Lock Screen, and Apple Watch.

It's free, it has no ads, and you don't need an account. A nonprofit builds it in partnership with your transit agency.

BEFORE YOU LEAVE

- See real-time arrivals at the stops around you, or search by route, address, or stop number.
- Bookmark the routes you ride every day and check them in one tap, from a widget, or on your wrist.
- Ask Siri, "When is my next bus?"
- Set an alarm that reminds you to head out before your bus arrives.
- Plan door-to-door trips across every agency in your region.

ON THE WAY

- Follow your ride from the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island with Live Activities.
- Watch your bus, train, ferry, or streetcar move on the map.
- Get an alert as you approach your stop, so you can relax instead of watching for it.
- Read service alerts from your agency, right on the stop and trip pages.
- Report a ghost bus when the app shows a vehicle that never arrives.

GETTING AROUND

- Find nearby bikes and scooters, in supporting regions.
- See on-demand and dial-a-ride services, with their booking deadlines.
- Turn on Bike Mode to estimate how long it takes to ride to a stop.

BUILT FOR EVERYONE

OneBusAway works with VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, and dark mode, and is translated into a dozen languages.
